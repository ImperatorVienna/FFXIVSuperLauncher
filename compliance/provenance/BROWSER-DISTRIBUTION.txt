Browser distribution review — Electron 44.5.1 Linux x64

Follow the upstream distribution boundary: package the pinned original Electron
Linux ZIP, retain LICENSE and LICENSES.chromium.html verbatim, replace only the
example app with our own packaging/webview shell, and add source-delivery notes.
Do not relabel Chromium's dependencies as MIT because Electron itself is MIT.

The original notice index has 779 nonempty product sections. Its SHA256 and
copyleft-text candidate list are in browser-notice-review.json. It is generated
for multiple platforms. Android/Windows/macOS entries alone do not prove those
components are shipped in this Linux package. Keep the full upstream index,
rather than deleting notices based on keyword guesses.

The Linux executable's ELF dependencies include libffmpeg.so, GLib/GIO/GObject,
NSPR/NSS, GTK, ATK, Cairo, CUPS, D-Bus, Pango, X11, GBM, Expat, xcb, xkbcommon,
udev, ALSA, AT-SPI, glibc and libgcc. In the current tar distribution libffmpeg.so
is bundled; the other listed system libraries are supplied by the host. This
distinction must be reviewed again if a future AppImage bundles host libraries.
Do not use this tar-package review as automatic approval of an AppImage closure.

The browser also includes upstream resources, SwiftShader and the Vulkan loader.
They retain the complete upstream notice index. No browser executable or shared
library was modified by the compliance work. The launcher shell uses the browser
as a separate process, not a C#-linked replacement for its license.

Electron's DEPS supplies Chromium and third-party revisions. Source recipients
must follow the upstream checkout/build instructions, including recursive DEPS
inputs; an Electron root-only archive is not a complete Chromium source checkout.
The exact FFmpeg revision/archive and shared-library hash are recorded separately
in webview.json, and its source archive is automatically attached to releases.
No full browser source build or per-object correspondence audit was performed.
Thus the source locations and intact upstream notices are evidenced, while a
claim that every embedded browser object was independently reconstructed is not.

If distributing additional browser-source copyleft components, ensure equivalent
source access remains available under their applicable terms; links are not an
excuse to ignore required source when an upstream location disappears. Preserve
the pinned release/DEPS records alongside each binary. The final AppImage review
must inventory any newly copied libraries and their source/notice requirements.
