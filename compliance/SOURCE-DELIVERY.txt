FFXIV Super Launcher — source delivery

For a binary release, provide every archive in SOURCE-DELIVERY.json through
links on the release page, without an additional charge. For 1.0.0, the unchanged
archives are stored in the repository under release-materials/1.0.0; the release
notes link to that directory. Do not rely solely on GitHub's
automatically generated source.zip: required injector submodules and third-party
source archives are separate materials. Preserve the archives and their hashes
for each distributed binary version. A downstream mirror must carry the sources
or provide equivalent access as allowed by the applicable licenses.

The launcher source archive includes build/packaging scripts, provenance, original
notices and the TC injector modification. The injector source archive includes
both upstream roots and their required FfxivArgLauncher submodule sources. Follow
BUILD.txt in that archive. Source matching and verification limits are recorded;
no byte-identical reconstruction of every upstream binary is claimed.

Reloaded.Memory, Reloaded.Memory.Buffers, Reloaded.Memory.Sigscan and its Definitions
assembly are unmodified LGPLv3 shared libraries used by the fixed CN helper.
Their version-source archives accompany the release. SigScan's archive contains
both Sigscan 3.0.0 and Definitions 1.0.0 projects. Retain the LGPL texts, as well as
the GPLv3 text at the root of this package. Users may modify/rebuild these libraries
and replace the corresponding DLLs under Tools/cn-launch-support/injector; this
project adds no license restriction on debugging such modifications. Packaging
hash checks identify the tested upstream inputs, not a grant to relicense them.
Upstream generated sources/build projects are retained. These are source archives,
not a complete offline NuGet/SDK mirror. See provenance/helper-dependencies.json.

Electron's original LICENSE and LICENSES.chromium.html remain under Tools/webview.
The separately supplied FFmpeg source matches the pinned Chromium revision;
Tools/webview/SOURCE-PROVENANCE.json records its hash and origin. libffmpeg.so is
a separate shared library and may be replaced with a compatible rebuilt version.
The application adds no restriction on reverse engineering for debugging LGPL
library modifications. Actual browser rebuilds have not been certified.

Runtime-downloaded game files, full Dalamud installations and user plugins are
not part of the launcher distribution. Their download sources and rights remain
separate. The two fixed bundled injector components ARE part of the distribution
and are covered by the explicit source delivery above.

Developer command: scripts/package-linux.sh now prepares these materials
automatically. SOURCE_MATERIAL_CACHE can contain colon-separated directories of
the pinned input archives; missing archives are downloaded from recorded public
upstream URLs and checked against SHA256 before use. Hash mismatches stop packaging.
This source delivery mechanism does not publish a GitHub release.
